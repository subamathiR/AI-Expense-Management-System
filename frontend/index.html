Autonomous Capabilities

Engineered for Precision & Financial Control

ExpenseAI unifies computer vision, deterministic policy checks, and smart workflows into a coherent corporate platform.

Intelligent Receipt OCR

Instantly scans receipt images and PDFs using client-side & server-side OCR. Automatically extracts vendor name, line items, transaction date, tax, and currency.

Smart Categorization

Machine intelligence classifies purchases across Travel, Meals, Office Supplies, and Software, learning corporate tax codes and departmental ledger rules.

Deterministic Policy Engine

Enforce spending limits per category, daily thresholds, and receipt requirements. Flag non-compliant submissions before they reach managers.

Fraud & Duplicate Guard

Identifies duplicate receipt uploads, matching vendor/date/amount combinations, and atypical spikes using explainable anomaly detection.

Dynamic Budget Tracking

Monitor department and category budgets in real-time. Receive proactive threshold alerts (e.g. 80% used) before team limits are breached.

Mileage & Route Tracking

Calculate accurate mileage reimbursements using start and destination coordinates or odometer logs based on IRS and corporate standard rates.

Streamlined Flow

From Receipt Snap to Settled Reimbursement

Three frictionless steps eliminate days of paperwork and tedious email approvals.

1

Snap or Drop Receipts

Upload single receipts, PDF invoices, or mileage trips on desktop or mobile. Our OCR engine parses vendor, amounts, taxes, and dates in seconds.

2

AI Audit & Verification

The rules engine cross-checks company limits, flags duplicates, applies currency conversion, and verifies tax splits with zero delay.

3

Multi-Tier Approval & Payout

Managers approve in one click from desktop or email. Finance teams monitor payment batches and sync directly with accounting software.
